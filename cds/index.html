What happens next
1. The desk checks your documents and calls you if anything needs fixing.
2. You sign the specimen signature card and the Client CDS Agreement in ink before a CRDB officer — the one step that can't be done online.
3. CRDB lodges the application; the Bank of Tanzania opens complete accounts within about 2 working days.
4. Your CDS ID and client account number are relayed to you within 3 working days of confirmation.
