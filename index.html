About The Ticker

The Ticker condenses the Bank of Tanzania's public releases — MPC statements, auction results, the issuance calendar and monthly economic data — into one page, translated into plain language. Every figure links back to its official source. It is an educational summary, not investment advice; always confirm against the official BOT publication before acting.
